Roobet proof of address

How to respond safely if your authenticated account asks for address evidence—without guessing the required document or sharing it with the wrong person.

By HowToKYC Editorial Team · Reviewed 6 November 2026 · Independent guide

Read the exact request first. Roobet’s public Level 2 article lists government ID categories, but an additional proof-of-address request can be account specific. The message inside your account or official support response controls what is required.

Confirm that the request is genuine

Sign in by typing the official Roobet address yourself and open the verification area. Do not follow a document-upload link from Telegram, Discord, email or a direct message. If the account message is unclear, contact support through the official Help Center.

Do not assume a utility bill, bank statement or government letter will automatically qualify. Document categories, issue-date limits and formatting requirements can vary. Use the category and date window shown in the request.

Prepare a readable document

  • Use a document issued to your real legal name.
  • Make sure the full address and issue date are visible.
  • Capture the complete page or document edges.
  • Avoid glare, blur, screenshots of edited copies and compressed messaging-app images.
  • Use the accepted file format and size displayed by the upload form.

Do not alter a document to create a match. A modified image can create a larger compliance problem than the original mismatch.

If the address does not match

Ask official support how to correct an honest account error or document a recent move. Do not open another account, borrow another person’s bill or enter a false location. Regional eligibility and identity records are separate issues, and false data does not safely resolve either one.

Never use proof of address to bypass a restricted region. Check Roobet’s current restricted-regions guidance and the laws that apply where you are located.

Submit documents safely

Use the authenticated account workflow. HowToKYC, affiliates and community moderators cannot approve verification and should never receive your identity files, password, 2FA code or wallet recovery information.

Official starting points: Roobet Level 2 Verification and the Roobet Help Center.

Proof-of-address FAQ

Can an affiliate tell me which bill will be accepted?

No. Only the operator can confirm the document category and account-specific requirement.

Should I cover transaction balances?

Follow the exact upload instructions. Do not edit or conceal fields unless official guidance explicitly permits it.

What if I recently moved?

Contact official support and ask what current evidence is accepted rather than changing the document or account details yourself.
