Identity and account safety

Roobet KYC security checklist

Protect your identity document, login and two-factor authentication before, during and after a verification review.

By HowToKYC Editorial Team · Reviewed 28 October 2026 · Independent guide

The essential rule: submit identity documents only through the verification area reached from your own official account. An affiliate, streamer, community moderator or direct-message contact cannot approve KYC and should never request a copy of your ID.

Before you upload an identity document

Confirm that account registration is permitted in your location and that the details entered on the account accurately identify you. Do not use another person’s document, a false address, a VPN or a second account to work around eligibility or verification rules.

Open the verification page by signing in through the website address you already trust. Avoid beginning a sensitive upload from a link in a social-media message, sponsored advertisement or unsolicited email.

  • Check the domain before signing in.
  • Use a private device that receives current security updates.
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi when transmitting identity information.
  • Close screen-sharing and remote-access software.
  • Make sure your account email is protected by a unique password and 2FA.

Upload documents through the official account area

Roobet’s current Level 2 instructions list a passport, driving licence or government-issued identification document and direct users to the verification section of account settings. The compliance team reviews submissions and may request additional information after manual review.

Primary source: Roobet Help Center — Level 2 Verification. Requirements can change; follow the instructions displayed in your account.

Prepare a clear, current image with the complete document visible. Follow the upload form when it requests front and back. Do not add filters, digitally alter information or publish the image to ask a community for help.

Do not send KYC files through Telegram, Discord or social media. If somebody claims they can fast-track or guarantee approval, stop the conversation and open the official Help Center independently.

Protect your password and two-factor authentication

A verification document is only one part of account security. Use a password that is not reused on another website. Protect the associated email account as carefully as the casino account because password-reset messages may be sent there.

Roobet’s official 2FA guide explains that an authenticator code changes every 30 seconds. It also warns users to save the secret recovery key and not share it because the key can be used to remove 2FA.

  • Store the 2FA recovery key offline in a private location.
  • Never paste an authenticator code into chat.
  • Never share a password-reset code or email verification code.
  • Do not disclose a crypto-wallet recovery phrase or private key.
  • Review active sessions and account notifications when available.

Security source: Roobet Help Center — 2FA Guide.

How to recognize the correct support path

Account-specific verification questions should be handled through the official Help Center and authenticated account workflow. An affiliate may explain publicly available requirements, but cannot see a compliance queue, approve a file, change account records or promise a decision.

Be cautious when a message creates urgency, requests documents outside the account, asks for cryptocurrency to unlock verification, or promises a guaranteed result. Navigate to the Help Center yourself rather than relying on the message link.

RequestSafe response
“Send your ID to me in chat”Do not send it; use the official verification area.
“Share your 2FA or recovery key”Refuse and secure the account immediately.
“Pay to guarantee approval”Stop contact and report the impersonation through official support.

After submitting or completing KYC

Keep monitoring the status inside your account. If more information is requested, read the exact message and respond through the designated official workflow. Delete unnecessary duplicate photographs from shared folders, messaging apps and devices you do not control, while retaining any records you are legally or operationally required to keep.

If you think credentials or a recovery key were disclosed, secure the associated email, change the account password through the official site, review 2FA and contact official support. Do not continue a conversation with the person who requested the information.

Security FAQ

Can a VIP host or affiliate approve my KYC?

No. Affiliates and community teams cannot approve identity documents or override a compliance review.

Where should I upload my ID?

Use only the verification section reached from your official account and follow the instructions displayed there.

Is it safe to share a 2FA recovery key with support?

No. Keep it private. Roobet’s own guide warns that the recovery key can be used to remove 2FA.

What if I already sent a document to an impersonator?

Stop contact, secure your email and account, preserve evidence of the conversation and contact official support. Consider identity-protection steps appropriate to your country.
