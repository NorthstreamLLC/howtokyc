Feature eligibility

Roobet KYC requirements for tips

Level 2 is required to send or receive tips, but verification and wagering are separate checks. Here is what that distinction means.

Reviewed 30 September 2026 · Independent guide

Short answer: Roobet’s current Help Center says accounts must complete Level 2 verification to send or receive tips. A received tip also carries a separate wagering requirement before withdrawal. Completing KYC does not remove that requirement.

Why Level 2 matters for tips

Roobet’s published “Sending Tips and Free Spins” instructions state that Level 2 must be completed to be eligible to tip or receive tips. The tipping screen asks for the recipient’s username, currency and amount. Entering the correct username matters because blockchain-style transfers and account tips should not be treated as reversible.

Level 2 currently requires an accepted government ID. Read our complete Level 2 guide or document checklist before uploading.

Feature source: Roobet Help Center — Sending Tips and Free Spins, checked 30 September 2026.

KYC and wagering are separate checks

Identity verification establishes account information and eligibility for particular features. It does not turn a tip into immediately withdrawable funds. Roobet’s current wagering-requirements article says received tips must be wagered at 30% of the tip amount before withdrawal under its AML policy.

For illustration, 30% of a $100 tip is $30 of required wagering. That is arithmetic, not a promise about balances, eligible games, outcomes or withdrawal approval. Check the amount and progress shown in the account because terms can change.

Wagering source: Roobet Help Center — Wagering Requirements, checked 30 September 2026.

Never wager to “unlock” money based only on a stranger’s message. Confirm the tip inside your own account and read the current requirements. Gambling can lose the entire balance.

Before sending or receiving a tip

  1. Confirm that the account and region are eligible and that Level 2 shows as completed.
  2. Navigate to the tip feature from your signed-in account rather than a link sent in chat.
  3. Verify the exact recipient username, currency and amount before confirming.
  4. Keep screenshots free of balances, email addresses and other private information if asking for help.
  5. For a received tip, check the displayed wagering progress before attempting withdrawal.
  6. Contact official support for account-specific errors; an affiliate cannot enable the feature.

Common tip and KYC scams

No legitimate helper needs your password, two-factor code, identity image or wallet recovery phrase to send a tip. Do not pay an “activation fee” to a social-media account, and do not let another person sign in to complete verification.

A community moderator may explain public rules, but only Roobet can review KYC, confirm feature eligibility or investigate a missing transfer. If a tip does not appear, collect the time, sender and recipient usernames, currency and public transaction details without exposing credentials.

What Level 2 does not guarantee

  • It does not guarantee a tip will be sent or received.
  • It does not guarantee immediate withdrawal.
  • It does not override regional restrictions or account reviews.
  • It does not guarantee promotion, VIP or free-spin eligibility.
  • It does not make wagering profitable or predictable.

Tips and KYC FAQ

Can I receive a Roobet tip without Level 2?

Roobet’s current public instructions say Level 2 must be completed to be eligible to send or receive tips.

Does a received tip have to be wagered?

Roobet’s current wagering article says received tips must be wagered at 30% of the tip amount before withdrawal.

Can SlotEssentials or an affiliate enable tipping?

No. Account verification and feature access are controlled by Roobet. Use official support for account-specific help.
