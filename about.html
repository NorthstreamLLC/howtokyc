About this site

Independent KYC guidance

HowToKYC explains Roobet eligibility, identity verification and account-safety topics using current official sources and plain language.

Reviewed 1 October 2026

Important: HowToKYC is an independent affiliate guide. We are not Roobet, a regulator, a law firm or a verification provider. We cannot access accounts, review identity documents or approve KYC.

What we publish

Our guides cover regional eligibility, Level 2 verification, accepted identity-document categories, review timing, troubleshooting and account security. The goal is to help readers reach the correct official process and avoid common risks such as fake support accounts, borrowed documents, false location information and unverified promises.

Requirements can change and can vary by account. For that reason, every guide points readers back to the current Roobet Help Center or the instructions displayed inside their own authenticated account.

How information is checked

We prioritize first-party materials: Roobet Help Center articles, account instructions, terms, security documentation and responsible-gambling resources. We distinguish facts stated by the operator from practical preparation advice. Pages show a review date so readers can judge how recently the cited requirements were checked.

Read the full editorial policy for our source hierarchy, correction process and affiliate standards.

Affiliate relationship

Some links use the ELITE referral code. HowToKYC or its associated SlotEssentials network may earn a commission when an eligible reader uses an affiliate link, at no extra cost to the reader. That relationship does not allow us to approve verification, change account eligibility or guarantee a promotion.

Affiliate links are labelled for search engines as sponsored. Our safety guidance remains the same whether or not a reader uses a referral link: check regional eligibility first, use accurate personal information, read the current terms and never gamble with money needed for living expenses.

What you should never send us

Do not send HowToKYC, SlotEssentials or a community moderator copies of your passport, driving licence, government ID, proof of address, passwords, authentication codes, wallet recovery phrases or 2FA recovery keys. Submit identity files only through the official verification area in your own account.

Corrections and account help

For account-specific questions, contact the official Roobet Help Center. General editorial corrections can be raised through the SlotEssentials community, but do not include account credentials or personal documents. We review supported corrections against primary sources and update the affected page when needed.
