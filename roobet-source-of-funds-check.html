Roobet source-of-funds check

A source-of-funds request is not the same as ordinary identity verification. Respond to the exact account request with accurate evidence and no invented explanation.

What “source of funds” means

The request is generally asking where the money or crypto used on the account came from. It is different from proving identity or address. An operator may request further evidence as part of anti-money-laundering and account-risk controls.

Roobet’s published wagering-requirements article states that deposits must be wagered before withdrawals or tips under its AML policy. That rule is separate from any additional review and does not guarantee that completing wagering closes a source-of-funds request.

Policy source: Roobet Help Center — Wagering Requirements.

Prepare a clear evidence trail

  • Read the requested date range and document category.
  • Use records belonging to you and matching the account identity.
  • Keep names, dates, account references and relevant amounts readable.
  • Provide an accurate explanation when the workflow asks for one.
  • Keep copies of what you submit and the submission date.

Examples may include employment, business, savings or investment records, but the operator—not this guide—decides what is acceptable for a specific review.

Crypto deposits and transaction records

Keep the wallet transaction hash, originating wallet or exchange record, asset, network and date. Roobet’s deposit guidance warns that only supported assets and networks should be used. A blockchain transaction alone may show movement of funds without establishing their original source, so follow any request for additional records.

Deposit source: Roobet Help Center — Cryptocurrency Deposits.

Do not make the review worse

Do not edit statements, borrow records, fabricate income, move money solely to create a cleaner trail or pay someone promising approval.

Do not send financial evidence to an affiliate, Telegram account or community moderator. Use the authenticated account process and official Help Center. If the request affects significant funds or presents a legal or tax question, consider independent professional advice in your jurisdiction.

Source-of-funds FAQ

Does completing KYC prevent this request?

Not necessarily. Identity verification and an additional source-of-funds review address different questions.

Can I withdraw while the review is open?

Account restrictions are case specific. Check the authenticated status and ask official support rather than relying on a third party.

How long does review take?

There is no reliable universal timeframe. Submit complete evidence once and use official support for account-specific status.
