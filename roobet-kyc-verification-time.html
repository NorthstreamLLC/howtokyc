Review timeline

How long does Roobet verification take?

There is no reliable universal countdown. Here is what the official guidance confirms, what can affect a manual review, and how to follow up without exposing your account.

Reviewed 9 September 2026 · Independent guide

Short answer: Roobet’s Level 2 instructions do not promise a fixed completion time. They state that the compliance team reviews verification documents daily and will reach out after manual review if anything further is required.

What happens after submission

After you submit the requested identity document in your account, the compliance team reviews it. A manual check may finish without another request, or the team may ask for additional information. Because accounts and compliance checks differ, somebody else’s timeline is not a promise for yours.

Primary source: Roobet Help Center — Level 2 Verification.

What can make verification take longer?

Review time can be affected when the submitted file is difficult to read, the front or back is missing, the document is expired, or account information does not match the document. Further compliance questions can also extend the process. These are general factors to check—not a definitive explanation for the status of any specific account.

  • Confirm the upload completed successfully in the official account area.
  • Check whether a message requests another document or clearer image.
  • Make sure your legal name and date of birth were entered accurately.
  • Check spam or junk folders for legitimate account communications.
  • Avoid repeatedly opening new support conversations for the same issue.

What should you do while waiting?

Keep the original documents available and monitor the status inside your account. Do not send duplicate files to social-media accounts or people claiming they can “fast-track” verification. Do not pay a third party to approve KYC; only the operator’s compliance process can decide the result.

Never gamble around a pending review. Do not open another account, use another person’s details or change your location with a VPN. If your location is restricted, do not attempt to bypass the restriction.

When should you contact official support?

If your account displays an error, requests information you do not understand, or remains unchanged beyond the expectation shown in your account, contact Roobet through its official Help Center. Include a concise description and any non-sensitive reference number. Do not publish your identity document or account security information in a community channel.

HowToKYC, SlotEssentials and referral-code operators cannot view a review queue, approve documents or guarantee when a decision will be made.

Verification-time FAQ

Is Roobet KYC instant?

Do not assume it is. Official guidance describes compliance-team review and the possibility of a follow-up request.

Should I upload the same document again?

Only when your account or official support asks you to do so. Repeated unsolicited submissions may not resolve the underlying issue.

Can a VIP host or affiliate approve verification?

No. Account verification is handled through Roobet’s official compliance and support processes.
