Level 2 guide

Roobet Level 2 verification

The current official requirements, a safe upload checklist, and what Level 2 does—and does not—mean for your account.

By HowToKYC Editorial Team · Reviewed 1 October 2026 · Independent guide

Short answer: Roobet’s current Level 2 instructions list a passport, driving licence or government-issued identification document. Upload through the verification area in your own account. The compliance team reviews documents and may request more information.

What Roobet publishes for Level 2

Roobet’s Help Center currently lists three accepted identity-document categories: a passport, driving licence, or government-issued identification document. Its instructions direct users to the verification page in account settings and say identification documentation should show the front and back where applicable.

This is a summary of the public Level 2 article, not a promise that one upload will be enough for every account. Compliance checks can depend on the document, account information and review. Follow the prompts displayed in your account if they differ from a general guide.

Primary source: Roobet Help Center — Level 2 Verification, checked 30 September 2026.

Level 2 verification steps

  1. Confirm that Roobet is legally available where you live. Do not use a VPN or false address to bypass restrictions.
  2. Sign in by navigating to Roobet yourself and open Account Settings → Verification.
  3. Select the requested document type and use a current document that belongs to you.
  4. Capture every requested side with all edges visible, even lighting and readable text.
  5. Check that your legal name and date of birth match the account information before submitting.
  6. Wait for the account message or official support response. Avoid sending duplicate files unless requested.

What manual review means

Roobet says its compliance team reviews verification documents daily and will contact the account holder if anything further is required. That does not establish a guaranteed completion time. “Reviewed daily” describes the review operation; it does not mean every case is approved within a day.

If more information is requested, read the exact message before uploading again. A request for another image is not necessarily a final rejection. The reviewer may need a clearer file, a missing side or additional information specific to the account.

Use our verification-time guide for waiting and escalation, or the troubleshooting guide for common upload and account-detail issues.

Protect your identity documents

Upload files only in the official account verification area. Affiliates, streamers, Telegram contacts and Discord moderators cannot approve Level 2 verification and should not receive your ID. Never share a password, two-factor code or wallet recovery phrase with someone offering to “speed up” KYC.

Do not alter or borrow documents. Filters, edited details, another person’s ID, a false address or VPN use can create compliance and account-access problems. If the service is unavailable in your region, do not register.

What Level 2 can be required for

Roobet’s current tipping instructions say Level 2 must be completed to send or receive tips. That is a feature requirement, not a guarantee that every other account feature is enabled, that a withdrawal will be approved, or that a promotion will be available. Each feature can have additional rules.

Level 2 FAQ

Which IDs are listed for Level 2?

A passport, driving licence or government-issued identification document, according to Roobet’s current public article.

Does Level 2 guarantee account approval?

No. Compliance teams may request additional information, and account or feature eligibility can involve separate rules.

Can an affiliate review my ID?

No. Submit identity documents through your official account and use Roobet’s official support for account-specific questions.
