Publishing standards

Editorial policy

The standards used to research, write, update and correct HowToKYC guidance.

Effective 1 October 2026

Primary-source first

For operator requirements, we prefer the current Roobet Help Center, account instructions, terms, security guidance and responsible-gambling documentation. We link directly to the relevant source whenever it supports a material claim. Third-party pages may help identify a topic, but they do not replace the operator's current policy.

Facts, guidance and uncertainty

Operator-published facts are attributed to the source. Practical advice—such as photographing an ID in even lighting or avoiding unsolicited support messages—is presented as preparation or safety guidance, not as a guarantee of approval. We do not invent fixed review times, approval odds or account-specific requirements when the official source does not publish them.

We clearly state when requirements can vary by account, jurisdiction or compliance review. Readers are directed to the authenticated account workflow or official support for individual decisions.

Review dates and material updates

Each guide displays a reviewed date. A page is materially updated when an official requirement changes, a source is replaced, a safety issue is corrected, or the page's main answer changes. Sitemap and structured-data modification dates are updated when the page receives a substantive review.

Identity-document safety

HowToKYC does not ask readers to upload KYC documents. Writers, affiliates and community moderators cannot approve verification. Pages must direct sensitive files to the official account process and warn against sharing IDs, passwords, authentication codes, wallet phrases or recovery keys through community channels.

Affiliate links and editorial independence

Some links use the ELITE referral code and may generate a commission. Affiliate links are marked as sponsored for search engines and disclosed to readers. Commission does not change our eligibility, document-safety or responsible-gambling guidance. We do not promise approval, guaranteed promotions or ways around regional restrictions.

Corrections

When a factual error is identified, we compare the claim with the most current primary source, correct the affected text and refresh the review date when the correction is material. General correction requests may be raised through the SlotEssentials community. Never include personal identity documents, account passwords or authentication codes.

Account-specific support: HowToKYC cannot view a verification queue or change a compliance decision. Use the official Roobet Help Center.
