Safe troubleshooting

Roobet verification rejected

A rejection is not a reason to upload random alternatives. Identify the stated problem, correct that issue once and use official support for the next permitted step.

By HowToKYC Editorial Team · Reviewed 6 November 2026 · Independent guide

Start with the authenticated message. “Rejected” can describe an unreadable image, unsupported document, mismatched details, expired ID, eligibility issue or a request for further review. The account message matters more than a generic checklist.

Check the submission against official requirements

Roobet’s Level 2 guidance lists a passport, driving licence or government-issued identification document and states that front-and-back images are required where applicable. Compare the rejected upload with the instructions displayed in your account.

  • Document is current and belongs to the account holder.
  • Legal name and date of birth match the account details.
  • All edges and required sides are visible.
  • Text and photograph are sharp, with no glare.
  • The image is original rather than edited or heavily compressed.
  • The selected document category matches the file.

Primary source: Roobet Help Center — Level 2 Verification.

Correct the stated issue once

If the workflow permits another upload, replace the file only after fixing the stated problem. Repeatedly submitting the same unreadable or unsupported image can slow resolution. If personal details contain an honest error or a legal name change, ask support how to document it instead of editing the ID.

Use official support for an unclear rejection

Roobet says its compliance team reviews Level 2 documents daily and may contact users after manual review if more is needed. That does not create a guaranteed completion time. Use the official Help Center and provide the submission date plus the exact non-sensitive error text.

Do not publish account screenshots containing personal data. Never give an affiliate or community moderator access to the account.

What not to do

Do not create a second account, use another person’s ID, alter a document, fake a location, pay for “verified accounts” or use a VPN to bypass eligibility.

If the rejection reflects a restricted region or an operator decision, this guide cannot reverse it. Check the current policy and local law before taking any further action.

Rejected-verification FAQ

How many times should I resubmit?

Follow the authenticated workflow. Fix the identified issue before another attempt and ask official support if repeated submission is not clearly permitted.

Can HowToKYC contact compliance for me?

No. We are independent and cannot access, approve or escalate accounts.

Does manual review mean rejection?

No. It means an account-specific human review may be needed. Wait for the official request and provide only what it asks for.
