Privacy and safety

Privacy notice

What this informational website measures, where outbound links lead and what sensitive information you must never submit here.

Effective 1 October 2026

Never send identity documents to HowToKYC. This site has no KYC upload form and cannot process verification. Submit requested documents only through the official verification area in your own account.

Information collected through normal website use

HowToKYC is an informational website. Like most websites, its hosting and analytics services can process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location and timestamps. This information is used to understand site performance, detect technical problems and improve content.

Google Analytics

The main page uses Google Analytics to measure visits and page interactions. Google may use cookies or similar technologies subject to its own policies. You can limit cookies through your browser settings or use Google's available opt-out controls. Blocking analytics does not prevent access to the written guides.

No document-processing service

HowToKYC does not provide account verification and does not need your passport, driving licence, government ID, proof of address, password, 2FA code, recovery key, wallet seed phrase or financial credentials. Do not include this information in a community message or correction request.

Outbound and affiliate links

Links can lead to Roobet, its Help Center, SlotEssentials, Telegram, Discord, responsible-gambling resources or other external services. Those services control their own privacy and security practices. Some Roobet links contain the ELITE affiliate reference and may generate a commission. Opening an external link can disclose standard referral information to the destination.

Community channels

If you choose to contact a Telegram or Discord community, your interaction is governed by that platform and the information you choose to disclose. Community moderators cannot approve KYC. Use official support for account-specific matters and never post sensitive personal or security information.

Children and legal age

This website is intended for adults of legal gambling age. It does not knowingly provide gambling referrals to children. Do not register with an operator unless you meet the age and location rules that apply to you.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when analytics, hosting or site features change. The effective date above identifies the latest review. For account privacy questions, contact the operator through its official support channels.
